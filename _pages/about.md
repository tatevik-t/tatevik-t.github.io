I am Tatevik, a PhD student and AI researcher with interests in LLMs, safe AI, and interpretability. I completed an M.Sc. in Applied Mathematics and Informatics at Russian-Armenian University, with thesis work in knowledge boundary detection and hallucination mitigation via uncertainty calibration in large language models.

LLM safety &middot; Mechanistic interpretability &middot; Uncertainty estimation and calibration &middot; Hallucination mitigation and abstention &middot; Multilingual NLP
